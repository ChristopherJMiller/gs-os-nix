# Hardware toolchain and bus carrier -- Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Prove the agent's PCB toolchain on a real board: a Nix-packaged KiCad 10 / SKiDL / Freerouting flow with mutation-proven `hw-*` gates, and the 2-layer Apple II bus carrier for an iCESugar-Pro, designed through that flow to JLCPCB fab outputs and renders for the owner's review.

**Architecture:** The board is Python. SKiDL subcircuits in `hw/src/` and a per-board top (`hw/carrier/top.py`) produce a netlist, a readable schematic and an `.lpf`, all driven by one pin map (`hw/carrier/pins.toml`). Scripts in `hw/tools/` turn the netlist into a placed `.kicad_pcb`, route it through Freerouting, render it and export fab files. The routed board is committed source. Each gate is a `hw/tools/check_*.py` with a `--selftest` that plants violations, wired into `nix flake check` from `nix/hw.nix`.

**Tech Stack:** KiCad 10.0.6 (`kicad-small`, the SWIG `pcbnew` module, `kicad-cli`), SKiDL 3.0.0 + `schematizer`, Freerouting 2.4.1, KiKit 1.8.1, Interactive HTML BOM 2.12.0, trellis's ECP5 database, Python 3 (nixpkgs' default, which KiCad's bindings are built for), pytest, Nix flakes, verilator (lint only).

**Spec:** `docs/HARDWARE.md` (HW-1..HW-12). This is plan 1 of 3 for its stage 2. Plan 2: the core's `mem_ready` and the shared clock constant (HW-7, HW-8). Plan 3: board gateware -- `a2bus_phy`, capture, SDRAM, the carrier bitstream, bring-up (HW-9).

## Global Constraints

- Branch `hw/toolchain-carrier` in its own git worktree, created with superpowers:using-git-worktrees (HW-5). Outside `hw/`, `nix/hw.nix`, `nix/pkgs/` and `rtl/board/`, the only files touched are `flake.nix` (three small hunks) and `docs/HARDWARE.md`. Never stage the software agent's files.
- KiCad stays 10.x (`kicad-small` from the flake's pinned nixpkgs); no KiCad 11 (HW-2).
- Licences (HW-10): design sources (`hw/src/`, `hw/carrier/` except tools, `hw/lib/`) carry `SPDX-License-Identifier: CERN-OHL-S-2.0`; `hw/LICENSE` is the CERN-OHL-S-2.0 text. Tools, tests and RTL carry `SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later`.
- No third-party footprints or symbols are vendored. A footprint missing from KiCad's library is generated by `hw/tools/footprints.py`.
- Nets come only from pads and the netlist, never from net names written onto tracks (HARDWARE.md "How the agent designs a board").
- Every gate has a `--selftest` whose planted violation must fail it, as `scripts/check-opcodes.py` does (D14). Each prints `HW <NAME> SELFTEST OK` and then `HW <NAME> OK`; the Nix check greps for both.
- Outline: ≤ 177.8 mm (7.00") long, ≤ 69.85 mm (2.75") above the finger tab, 1.6 mm FR-4, 2 layers, ENIG, beveled gold fingers (JLCPCB: 30° or 45°, no copper in the bevel zone).
- Fingers: 25 per side at 2.54 mm; pins 1-25 on F.Cu, 26-50 on B.Cu; pin n opposite pin 51 − n; pin 1 toward the computer's front.
- Power: slot +5 V reaches nothing but the polyfuse and its own bypass capacitor; target ≤ 300 mA at +5 V; +12 V, −5 V, −12 V each get a bypass capacitor to GND and nothing else.
- Every slot-facing driver is off at power-up: translator `/OE` pulled up to +3V3, `DIR` pulled down (slot → FPGA); open-drain buffer inputs pulled up (released). The reset-hold buffer's input is pulled down (holding /RESET), with jumper `JP2` to +3V3 that disables the hold.
- Debug: shrouded 2×5 1.27 mm Cortex-10 JTAG header (pin 1 VTref = +3V3, nRESET unconnected); 1×3 2.54 mm UART header (TX, RX, GND); a SAFE jumper on `safe_n` (pulled up, the jumper grounds it).
- Fab target: JLCPCB. Every part with a footprint has an `LCSC` field matching `^C\d+$`. Through-hole headers and jumpers carry `Assembly = "hand"` and are left out of the CPL.
- Net names: power `+5V_SLOT`, `+5V` (after the fuse), `+3V3`, `GND`, `+12V_SLOT`, `-5V_SLOT`, `-12V_SLOT`. Slot-side signals: `SLOT_` + the slot name (`SLOT_A0`, `SLOT_D7`, `SLOT_DEVSEL_N`). FPGA-side nets carry the RTL port name, and a bus bit is `<port><i>` (`slot_a0`); the LPF names it `<port>[<i>]`.
- Hardware outputs are Linux-only: wrap every `nix/hw.nix` output in `lib.optionalAttrs pkgs.stdenv.isLinux`, so `nix flake check` still evaluates on Darwin.

## Review Focus

Each of these is pinned by a test in the task that owns it:

1. **Connector orientation.** If the fingers are reversed or mirrored, +5 V lands on a signal pin. ERC and DRC both pass such a board. → Task 3 footprint test; Task 7 `hw-edge`.
2. **Power-up state of every driver.** A translator enabled before the FPGA configures fights the bus. Nothing but a resistor shows this. → Task 5 `hw-intent`.
3. **Daisy chains.** If INT IN/OUT or DMA IN/OUT are not passed through, other cards' interrupts and DMA silently stop. → Task 5 `hw-intent`.
4. **Rails.** A 3.3 V buffer on +5 V would drive 5 V into the FPGA. Slot +5 V reaching a load without the fuse, or GND on a pin other than 26, are the same class of fault. → Task 5 and Task 6 `hw-intent`.
5. **Module pin transcription.** One swapped SODIMM pin passes every other check. → Task 4 consistency tests.

### Task 2: Netlist reader and pin map

**Files:**
- Create: `hw/tools/netlist.py`, `hw/tools/pins.py`, `hw/tests/test_netlist.py`, `hw/tests/test_pins.py`, `hw/tests/fixtures/tiny.net`, `hw/tests/fixtures/pins_ok.toml`, `hw/tests/fixtures/ports.v`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces in `hw/tools/netlist.py`:
  - `@dataclass(frozen=True) class PartInfo: value: str; footprint: str; fields: dict[str, str]`
  - `@dataclass class Netlist: parts: dict[str, PartInfo]; nets: dict[str, set[tuple[str, str]]]`. The second is net name → {(ref, pin number)}.
  - `def load(path: Path) -> Netlist`, which parses the KiCad s-expression netlist SKiDL writes.
  - `def net_of(nl: Netlist, ref: str, pin: str) -> str | None`
  - `def neighbours(nl: Netlist, net: str) -> set[str]`, returning the refs on a net.
  - `def through(nl: Netlist, ref: str, net: str) -> set[str]`: for a 2-pin part with one pin on `net`, the net on its other pin.
- Produces in `hw/tools/pins.py`:
  - `@dataclass class Port: name: str; dir: Literal["in","out","inout"]; balls: list[str]; io_standard: str; on_module: bool`
  - `@dataclass class PinMap: part: str; ports: dict[str, Port]`
  - `def load(path: Path) -> PinMap`
  - `def bits(p: Port) -> list[tuple[str, str, str]]`, returning `(net_name, lpf_name, ball)` per bit.
  - `def to_lpf(pm: PinMap) -> str`
  - `def verilog_ports(path: Path, module: str) -> dict[str, tuple[str, int]]`, port → (dir, width), from an ANSI port list.

pins.toml format, one table per port:

```toml
part = "LFE5U-25F-6BG256C"
io_standard = "LVCMOS33"          # default for every port
[ports.slot_a]
dir = "in"
balls = ["B3", "A3", …]           # 16 entries; index i is bit i
[ports.clk25]
dir = "in"
balls = ["P6"]
on_module = true                  # not on the SODIMM; skipped by connector checks
```

  - `SN74LVC8T245`: data. VCCA = `+3V3`, VCCB = `+5V`; `DIR` on `slot_d_dir`, `/OE` on `slot_d_oe_n`.
  - `74ALVC164245`: A0-A15. 1DIR and 2DIR on `slot_a_dir`; 1OE and 2OE on `slot_a_oe_n`.

  - Two `74LVC541`, VCC = `+3V3`:
    - listen-only `SLOT_D0..7` → `slot_dl0..7`;
    - the inputs `/DEVSEL /IOSEL /IOSTRB /M2SEL M2B0 /RESET /SYNC` → `slot_devsel_n slot_iosel_n slot_iostrb_n slot_m2sel_n slot_m2b0 slot_reset_n slot_sync_n`.
  - Two `74LVC2G17` for PH0, PH1, Q3, 7M, each through a series resistor footprint (0 Ω fitted).
  - Six `74LVC1G07` open-drain buffers: `slot_irq_n_o slot_nmi_n_o slot_inh_n_o slot_dma_n_o slot_rdy_o slot_reset_n_o` → /IRQ, /NMI, /INH, /DMA, RDY, /RESET.
  - `F1`: polyfuse `+5V_SLOT` → `+5V`, 500 mA hold.
  - 0 Ω links INT IN (28) → INT OUT (23) and DMA IN (27) → DMA OUT (24).
  - Pull resistors per Global Constraints, and `JP2`.
  - 100 nF on every IC supply pin; 10 µF on `+5V` and `+3V3`; 100 nF on each of `+12V_SLOT`, `-5V_SLOT`, `-12V_SLOT`.
  - 0603 passives.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write `check_intent.py` with its checks and mutations** (the failing gate). `problems()` reports each of these:
  - `+5V_SLOT` holds anything besides `J1.25`, `F1` and capacitors.
  - `J1.26` is not `GND`, or `GND` lands on any other `J1` pin.
  - `J1.33`, `J1.34` or `J1.50` reach anything but a capacitor whose other side is `GND`.
  - `J1.28` and `J1.23` (and `J1.27` and `J1.24`) are not joined directly or through a 0 Ω part.
  - `slot_d_oe_n` or `slot_a_oe_n` lacks a resistor through to `+3V3`.
  - `slot_d_dir` or `slot_a_dir` lacks a resistor through to `GND`.
  - Any of the five released open-drain inputs lacks a pull-up to `+3V3`.
  - `slot_reset_n_o` lacks a pull-down to `GND`, or `JP2` does not join it to `+3V3`.
  - A part valued `74LVC541`, `74LVC2G17` or `74LVC1G07` has any pin on `+5V` or `+5V_SLOT`.
  - A slot data pin (`J1.42`-`J1.49`) does not reach both a `74LVC541` and the `SN74LVC8T245`.
  - With `--full` (Task 6): the module's 5V pins are not on `+5V`, its 3V3 pins are not on `+3V3`, or its GND pins are not on `GND`.

  `MUTATIONS` has one entry per rule, each applied to a deep copy, for example `drop_oe_pullup`, `dir_pull_to_3v3`, `break_int_chain`, `541_on_5v` and `fuse_bypassed`. `--selftest` asserts that every mutation produces at least one problem.
